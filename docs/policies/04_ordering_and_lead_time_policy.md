# Ordering and Lead Time Policy

Document owner: Procurement and Supply Planning. Applies to all BUY, MAKE and TRANSFER sourced items of Northwind Industrial Systems (a fictional company used for this portfolio project).

## Purpose

This policy sets the rules for when and how much to order, so that supply arrives when the plan needs it without building unnecessary stock.

## Lead times

Every item carries a standard lead time in days. Typical lead times in this business:

- Class A items: 21 to 45 days
- Class B items: 14 to 28 days
- Class C items: 7 to 14 days

An order for a Class A item with a lead time longer than 28 days must be released at least one full week earlier than the arithmetic requires, to absorb supplier variability.

## Order release rules

An order is released in the week the plan calls for it, offset by the item's lead time. Planners do not release orders ahead of that week unless an expedite has been approved.

## Minimum order quantity

Order quantities are rounded up to the item's minimum order quantity (MOQ). When rounding up creates excess, the planner may accept it only if the surplus will be consumed within 8 weeks of forecast demand.

## Frozen window

Orders due to arrive within the next 2 weeks are frozen. They cannot be changed or cancelled without approval from the Supply Planning Manager.

## Supplier reliability

Items sourced from a supplier whose on-time delivery rate has fallen below 85 percent over the last quarter must carry an additional 0.5 weeks of cover until the rate recovers. The planner records this adjustment against the item.
