# Planning Cadence Policy

Document owner: Supply Planning. Applies to all planning teams of Northwind Industrial Systems (a fictional company used for this portfolio project).

## Purpose

A predictable planning rhythm keeps planners, buyers and plants working from the same plan.

## Planning horizon

The supply plan covers 52 weeks. The first 8 weeks are the execution horizon, where orders are released. Weeks 9 to 26 are the planning horizon, used for capacity and supplier signals. Weeks 27 to 52 are the strategic horizon, used for budgeting and long lead time commitments.

## Weekly cycle

- Monday: the new forecast version is published and the plan is regenerated.
- Tuesday: planners review new exceptions, starting with HIGH severity.
- Wednesday: the weekly supply planning meeting reviews escalated shortages and large excesses.
- Thursday: order changes agreed in the meeting are released to buyers and plants.
- Friday: planners close or update the status of every exception they reviewed that week.

## Forecast versions

Each Monday run produces a new forecast version labelled with the year and ISO week, for example 2026-W43. Planners compare the current version to the previous one to see what changed. A week over week change of more than 40 percent in an item's forecast must be explained in the exception notes.

## Quarter end

In the final two weeks of each quarter, order changes that move more than 10,000 dollars of spend between quarters require Finance approval.

## Holiday periods

Before a plant shutdown or public holiday period, planners review all orders due during the closure and move them to the nearest working week.
