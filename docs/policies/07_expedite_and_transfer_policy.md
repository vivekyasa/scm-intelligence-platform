# Expedite and Transfer Policy

Document owner: Supply Planning and Logistics. Applies to all expedite and inter-location transfer decisions at Northwind Industrial Systems (a fictional company used for this portfolio project).

## Purpose

Expediting and transferring stock solve shortages but cost money. This policy sets approval limits so that the cost of the fix stays proportionate to the cost of the problem.

## Expedite approval limits

The extra cost of an expedite is the premium paid above the standard price and freight.

- Up to 1,000 dollars: the planner decides.
- 1,000 to 5,000 dollars: the Supply Planning Manager must approve.
- Above 5,000 dollars: the Director of Operations must approve.

Expedites for Class A items with a HIGH shortage may be approved verbally by the Supply Planning Manager and documented within one business day.

## When to expedite

Expedite only when the expedited supply can actually arrive before the shortage week. If the supplier's fastest possible delivery is later than the shortage week, expediting does not help and the planner should consider a transfer or re-allocation instead.

## Transfers

A transfer between locations is preferred over an expedite when another location holds stock above 6 times its safety stock quantity for the same item. Transfers must not push the sending location below its own safety stock.

Transfers within the same region are approved by the planner. Transfers across regions need Supply Planning Manager approval because of freight cost and customs handling.

## Recording

Every expedite and transfer is recorded against the exception that triggered it, with the cost, the approver and the expected arrival date.
